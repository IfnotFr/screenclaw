# Google SERP

## Elements
- **Search bar**: top of page, contains the current query
- **Filter tabs**: below the header (All, Images, Videos, News…), active tab has a blue underline
- **Results**: vertical list of cards — blue title (clickable), green URL, grey snippet
- **Knowledge Panel**: right side, summary box — not a result, do not click it to navigate
- **Ads**: labeled "Sponsored", at the top and bottom of results — ignore unless explicitly needed

## Actions
- **Refine search**: click the search bar, edit the query, press Enter
- **Open a result**: first verify the **green URL** under the title matches the expected domain, then click the blue title
- **Go back to SERP**: `executeHotkey("alt+left")` — never try to locate the back button visually
- **Switch result type**: click a filter tab (Images, News…)
- **Load more results**: scroll down, or click "More results" if it appears
