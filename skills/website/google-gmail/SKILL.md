# EXPERT SKILL: Google Gmail

## Visual Anchors
- **SIDEBAR**: Located on the left. Contains "Compose", "Inbox", "Sent", "Drafts".
- **SEARCH BAR**: Top center. Primary tool for finding specific emails or senders.
- **ACTION BAR**: Top horizontal row (above email list). Appears when emails are selected. Contains "Archive", "Report Spam", "Delete", "Mark as unread".
- **EMAIL ROW**: Each email is a horizontal row. Anchors: Checkbox (far left), Star (next to checkbox), Sender name, Subject text, Timestamp (far right).

## Initial Preparation

**Before any action**, scan for floating compose windows (bottom-right area). If any are open:
- Click the **×** (close icon) at the top-right corner of each compose window.
- Repeat until no compose windows remain.

## Workflows

Strictly follow the workflows correponding to the actions you will perform on the interface.

#### 1. Composing an Email (Priority: Hotkey Navigation)
- **Action**: Click the "Compose" button (top left).
- **Behavior**: A "New Message" modal opens at the bottom-right with the **"To" field automatically focused**.
- **Reliability Rule**: Do NOT try to locate input fields by coordinates. Use the following keyboard sequence:
    1. **To**: Already focused. Type recipient then press `Enter` to validate it.
    2. **Subject**: Press `Tab` once from "To", then type subject.
    3. **Body**: Press `Tab` once from "Subject", then type content.
- **Send**: After typing the body, click the blue "Send" button at the bottom-left of the modal.
- **Verification**: If focus is lost, click the previous text you typed to regain focus on the current field before continuing.

#### 2. Reading and Replying
- **Action**: Click on the **Subject text** of an email row to open it.
- **Replying**: Use the floating button "Reply" at the bottom left of the screen. The reply textarea is automatically focused when you click the "Reply" button, so you can start typing immediately.
- **Sending the reply**: Click the blue "Send" button at the bottom-left of the reply modal.
